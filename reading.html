From darkness, rebirth

My three-card reading

I pulled three cards while reflecting on the four forces: Khaos, Nyx, Eos and Bennu. All three came out reversed.

Four of Pentacles

Reversed · Khaos

Release control, let go of what I’m holding onto, and make room for possibility.

Three of Pentacles

Reversed · Nyx

Look inward, question outside expectations, and discover my own path.

Ace of Wands

Reversed · Eos & Bennu

Don’t force a new beginning. Be patient with inspiration, growth, and transformation.

All three felt equally important, forming one connected message rather than separate answers.

Khaos reminds me to release.
Nyx reminds me to look within.
Eos reminds me to welcome the dawn.
Bennu reminds me that I can rise again.

I’m still learning what this connection means to me, but these four figures have become meaningful parts of my spiritual exploration.

ZERØ. A beginning, not an absence.
